The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 18,000 on or before the 5th of every month by bank transfer.
The security deposit of Rs. 1,08,000 shall be refunded after the Tenant vacates, less any deductions the Owner deems necessary.
The Owner may terminate this agreement by giving the Tenant ten days' notice.
The Tenant may terminate this agreement by giving two months' written notice to the Owner.
This agreement shall stand automatically renewed for eleven months unless the Tenant gives notice sixty days before expiry.
On renewal the rent shall be increased by 12% of the last rent paid.
Minor repairs shall be done by the Tenant; major and structural repairs shall be done by the Owner at his own cost.
The Owner may visit and inspect the flat at any time without prior notice.
Late payment shall attract interest at 2% per month on the amount due.
The Tenant shall keep the flat in good condition, normal wear and tear excepted.
Overnight guests are not permitted without the Owner's written approval.
Either party may refer any dispute to the Rent Authority having jurisdiction.
