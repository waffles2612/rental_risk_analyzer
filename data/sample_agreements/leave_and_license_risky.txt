LEAVE AND LICENSE AGREEMENT

This Leave and License Agreement is made and executed at Bengaluru on this 1st day of August 2026 between Mr. R. Prakash (hereinafter called the "Licensor") and Ms. A. Menon (hereinafter called the "Licensee").

WHEREAS the Licensor is the absolute owner of Flat No. 304, Green Meadows Apartments, and has agreed to grant the Licensee a licence to occupy the said premises on the following terms:

1. TERM: The licence is granted for a period of eleven (11) months commencing from 1st August 2026. This agreement shall be automatically renewed for a further period of eleven months unless the Licensee gives written notice at least ninety (90) days before expiry.

2. LICENSE FEE: The Licensee shall pay a monthly license fee of Rs. 25,000/- (Rupees Twenty Five Thousand only) on or before the 5th day of every month. Upon renewal the license fee shall be increased by 15% over the last paid license fee.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT: The Licensee has paid an interest-free security deposit of Rs. 2,50,000/- to the Licensor. The deposit shall be refunded after the Licensee vacates the premises, after deducting any amounts due, at the sole discretion of the Licensor.

4. A sum equivalent to one month's license fee shall be deducted from the security deposit towards painting and cleaning charges at the time of vacating, irrespective of the condition of the premises.

5. LOCK-IN: There shall be a lock-in period of eleven months. If the Licensee vacates during the lock-in period, the Licensee shall pay the license fee for the remaining lock-in period and the security deposit shall stand forfeited.

6. TERMINATION: The Licensor may terminate this agreement at any time without assigning any reason by giving fifteen (15) days notice to the Licensee. The Licensee may terminate this agreement by giving three (3) months notice in writing to the Licensor.

7. LATE PAYMENT: If the license fee is not paid by the due date, the Licensee shall pay a late fee of Rs. 500/- per day of delay along with interest at 3% per month on the outstanding amount.

8. REPAIRS: The Licensee shall carry out all repairs and maintenance of the premises at her own cost, including normal wear and tear, plumbing, electrical and structural repairs.

9. INSPECTION: The Licensor or his agents shall be entitled to enter and inspect the premises at any time without prior notice. The Licensor shall retain a duplicate set of keys to the premises.

10. GUESTS: The Licensee shall not allow any guests or visitors to stay overnight in the premises without the prior written permission of the Licensor.

11. INDEMNITY: The Licensee shall indemnify the Licensor against all losses, claims and damages of whatsoever nature arising in connection with the premises. The Licensor shall not be liable for any loss, theft or injury suffered by the Licensee or her guests.

12. TAXES: Property tax and all municipal taxes in respect of the premises shall be borne by the Licensee during the term of this agreement.

13. DEFAULT: In case of any default, the Licensor shall be entitled to disconnect the electricity and water supply and change the locks of the premises.

14. AMENDMENT: The Licensor may modify the rules and conditions of this agreement at any time by written intimation to the Licensee.

15. OVERSTAY: If the Licensee fails to vacate on expiry, she shall pay double the license fee for every month of overstay.

16. The Licensee hereby waives all rights to object to any deduction made from the security deposit.

17. The premises shall be used only for residential purposes by the Licensee and her immediate family. The Licensee shall not sublet or assign the premises.

18. Disputes arising under this agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of courts in Bengaluru.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have signed this agreement on the date first mentioned above.
