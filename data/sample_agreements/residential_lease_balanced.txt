RESIDENTIAL TENANCY AGREEMENT

This Tenancy Agreement is entered into on 10 September 2026 between Mrs. S. Iyer ("Landlord") and Mr. K. Thomas ("Tenant") for the residential premises at No. 12, 3rd Cross, Jayanagar, Bengaluru.

1. Term
1.1 The tenancy shall be for a period of twelve (12) months starting 1 October 2026. The Tenant shall have the option to renew the tenancy for a further twelve months on the same terms, subject to the rent increase in clause 2.3.

2. Rent
2.1 The monthly rent is Rs. 30,000, payable by bank transfer on or before the 5th of each month.
2.2 The Landlord shall issue a written receipt for every payment received.
2.3 On renewal, the rent may be increased by not more than 5% of the rent last paid.

3. Security Deposit
3.1 The Tenant shall pay a refundable security deposit of Rs. 60,000.
3.2 The deposit shall be refunded within fifteen (15) days of the Tenant handing over vacant possession, less any amounts for damage beyond normal wear and tear, supported by an itemised statement with bills.

4. Repairs and Maintenance
4.1 The Tenant shall carry out minor day-to-day repairs such as replacing bulbs and fuses.
4.2 The Landlord shall carry out all major and structural repairs, including roof, plumbing lines and wiring, within a reasonable time of being informed.
4.3 The Tenant shall keep the premises in good condition, normal wear and tear excepted.

5. Late Payment
5.1 If rent remains unpaid seven days after the due date (grace period), a one-time late fee of Rs. 500 shall be payable.

6. Access
6.1 The Landlord may enter the premises for inspection or repairs with at least 24 hours prior written notice to the Tenant, except in an emergency.

7. Termination
7.1 Either party may terminate this agreement by giving one month's written notice to the other party.
7.2 The Landlord may terminate the tenancy earlier only if the Tenant fails to pay rent for two consecutive months or materially breaches this agreement, after giving thirty days' written notice to remedy the breach.

8. Use of Premises
8.1 The premises shall be used for residential purposes only. The Tenant shall not sublet the premises without the Landlord's written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.
8.2 The Tenant may keep one domestic pet.

9. Liability
9.1 Each party shall be responsible only for loss or damage caused by its own negligence or breach of this agreement.

10. Utilities
10.1 Electricity and water charges as per meter readings shall be paid by the Tenant. Property tax shall be paid by the Landlord.

11. Disputes
11.1 Any dispute shall first be discussed in good faith, failing which either party may approach the Rent Authority or competent court.
