ROOM RENTAL AGREEMENT

This agreement is between the Owner, Mr. D. Rao, and the Tenant, Mr. V. Nair, for a furnished room in a shared flat in Koramangala.

Rent: The rent is Rs. 12,000 per month, payable in cash on the first day of every month. No receipts will be issued for cash payments.

Deposit: The tenant shall pay a deposit equal to three months' rent. A sum of Rs. 5,000 from the deposit is non-refundable and will be retained towards administrative charges.

The tenant must keep the room clean and is responsible for any damage to furniture provided in the room. Common areas will be cleaned by a housekeeper arranged by the owner.

The owner may revise the rent at any time as the owner deems fit, with intimation to the tenant.

Either party may end this arrangement with thirty days' notice.

The tenant shall not keep pets or smoke inside the flat. Loud music after 10 pm is not allowed.

Electricity charges will be split equally among all occupants of the flat.
