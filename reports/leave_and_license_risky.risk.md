# Risk report: leave and license risky

**Overall risk: High** · 19 clauses · 16 risky, 0 tenant-favourable · engine: hybrid

## Whole-agreement findings

- **Deposit is about 10.0× the monthly rent** (high). The security deposit equals roughly 10.0 months of rent. That is a lot of your money held by the landlord, and it is harder to recover in a dispute. For reference, India's Model Tenancy Act, 2021 suggests a cap of two months' rent for residential premises (it applies only in states that have adopted it). *Ask for:* Negotiate the deposit down to 2-3 months' rent, or ask for interest on the excess.
- **You must give more notice than the landlord** (high). You have to give about 90 days' notice to leave, but the landlord only has to give about 15 days. The termination terms are tilted against you. *Ask for:* Ask for the same notice period for both parties.

## Flagged clauses

### Clause 1 · Renewal & term · medium

> TERM: The licence is granted for a period of eleven (11) months commencing from 1st August 2026. This agreement shall be automatically renewed for a further period of eleven months unless the Licensee gives written notice at least ninety (90) days before expiry.

The agreement renews by itself unless someone gives notice in time. Miss the window and you can be bound for another term, often at a higher rent. Key numbers: 3 month(s), 11 month(s).

*Ask for:* Put the notice deadline in your calendar, or ask for renewal only by mutual written agreement.

### Clause 2 · Rent & payment · high

> The Licensee shall pay a monthly license fee of Rs. 25,000/- (Rupees Twenty Five Thousand only) on or before the 5th day of every month. Upon renewal the license fee shall be increased by 15% over the last paid license fee.

The rent rises by more than 10%, above the increases customary on renewal (often around 5-10%). Key numbers: ₹25,000, 15%.

*Ask for:* Negotiate a lower cap (e.g. 5%) or a fixed rent for the first renewal.

### Clause 3 · Security deposit · high

> The Licensee has paid an interest-free security deposit of Rs. 2,50,000/- to the Licensor. The deposit shall be refunded after the Licensee vacates the premises, after deducting any amounts due, at the sole discretion of the Licensor.

This clause lets one side decide on its own, with no objective standard and no way for you to challenge the decision. The security deposit equals roughly 10.0 months of rent. That is a lot of your money held by the landlord, and it is harder to recover in a dispute. For reference, India's Model Tenancy Act, 2021 suggests a cap of two months' rent for residential premises (it applies only in states that have adopted it). Key numbers: ₹250,000.

*Ask for:* Replace 'sole discretion' with an objective test (e.g. 'reasonable, documented costs') or require mutual agreement.

### Clause 4 · Security deposit · medium

> A sum equivalent to one month's license fee shall be deducted from the security deposit towards painting and cleaning charges at the time of vacating, irrespective of the condition of the premises.

A fixed charge (often painting or cleaning) comes out of your deposit whatever condition you leave the home in. Repainting after normal use is usually ordinary wear and tear. You must pay for repainting whatever state the home is in. Repainting after normal use is usually the owner's cost. Key numbers: 1 month(s).

*Ask for:* Make painting/cleaning deductions conditional on actual damage, with receipts.

### Clause 5 · Termination & notice · high

> There shall be a lock-in period of eleven months. If the Licensee vacates during the lock-in period, the Licensee shall pay the license fee for the remaining lock-in period and the security deposit shall stand forfeited.

The landlord can keep the whole deposit as a penalty, not just the cost of actual damage or unpaid rent. If you leave early you must pay rent for the rest of the term, which can be a large lump sum. Key numbers: 11 month(s).

*Ask for:* Limit deductions to unpaid rent and documented repair costs; remove forfeiture.

### Clause 6 · Termination & notice · high

> The Licensor may terminate this agreement at any time without assigning any reason by giving fifteen (15) days notice to the Licensee. The Licensee may terminate this agreement by giving three (3) months notice in writing to the Licensor.

The tenancy can be ended at any time or without a reason, leaving you exposed to sudden loss of your home. The landlord can end the tenancy with under a month's notice, which is very little time to find a new home. Key numbers: 15 day(s), 3 month(s).

*Ask for:* Require a written notice period (at least 1 month) and limit landlord termination to specific breaches.

### Clause 7 · Penalties & late fees · high

> If the license fee is not paid by the due date, the Licensee shall pay a late fee of Rs. 500/- per day of delay along with interest at 3% per month on the outstanding amount.

Interest of 2% or more per month is 24%+ per year, far above normal lending rates. Charges that accrue every day add up quickly, especially if a payment is delayed by a bank holiday or an emergency. Key numbers: ₹500, 3%.

*Ask for:* Ask for a modest flat late fee or a low annual interest rate.

### Clause 8 · Repairs & maintenance · high

> The Licensee shall carry out all repairs and maintenance of the premises at her own cost, including normal wear and tear, plumbing, electrical and structural repairs.

Making you responsible for every repair includes major and structural problems that are normally the landlord's job. Structural repairs (roof, walls, main plumbing or wiring) are expensive and are normally the owner's responsibility.

*Ask for:* Split responsibilities: minor day-to-day repairs for you, major/structural repairs for the landlord.

### Clause 9 · Landlord entry & access · high

> The Licensor or his agents shall be entitled to enter and inspect the premises at any time without prior notice. The Licensor shall retain a duplicate set of keys to the premises.

The landlord can come into your home whenever they like, which affects your privacy and security. The landlord keeps a key to your home, so entry is possible without you knowing.

*Ask for:* Require at least 24 hours' written notice except in a genuine emergency.

### Clause 10 · Use of premises · medium

> The Licensee shall not allow any guests or visitors to stay overnight in the premises without the prior written permission of the Licensor.

You need permission to have guests, which reaches into your private life in your own home.

*Ask for:* Limit restrictions to long-term additional occupants, not ordinary visitors.

### Clause 11 · Liability & indemnity · medium

> The Licensee shall indemnify the Licensor against all losses, claims and damages of whatsoever nature arising in connection with the premises. The Licensor shall not be liable for any loss, theft or injury suffered by the Licensee or her guests.

Words like 'whatsoever' or 'irrespective of the cause' make the obligation unlimited, including situations that are not your fault. You agree to cover the landlord's losses or claims broadly, possibly including ones you did not cause.

*Ask for:* Limit the obligation to losses caused by your own negligence or breach.

### Clause 12 · Rent & payment · medium

> Property tax and all municipal taxes in respect of the premises shall be borne by the Licensee during the term of this agreement.

Property/municipal tax is normally the owner's liability; this clause shifts it to you.

*Ask for:* Ask that property and municipal taxes stay with the owner.

### Clause 13 · Penalties & late fees · high

> In case of any default, the Licensor shall be entitled to disconnect the electricity and water supply and change the locks of the premises.

The landlord may cut utilities, change locks or remove your belongings. Evicting a tenant normally requires due legal process, not self-help.

*Ask for:* Delete this clause entirely.

### Clause 14 · Other · high

> The Licensor may modify the rules and conditions of this agreement at any time by written intimation to the Licensee.

One party can change the agreement after you sign it, so the deal you agreed to may not be the deal you get.

*Ask for:* Add: 'No change to this agreement is valid unless made in writing and signed by both parties.'

### Clause 15 · Termination & notice · high

> If the Licensee fails to vacate on expiry, she shall pay double the license fee for every month of overstay.

Overstaying or a breach costs double (or more) the rent, a penalty far larger than the landlord's actual loss.

*Ask for:* Replace with ordinary per-day rent for any overstay agreed in writing.

### Clause 16 · Security deposit · high

> The Licensee hereby waives all rights to object to any deduction made from the security deposit.

The clause asks you to waive rights or remedies you would otherwise have. Waivers like this are hard to undo once signed.

*Ask for:* Strike the waiver, or limit it to a specific, named situation.
