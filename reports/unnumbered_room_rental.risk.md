## Flagged clauses

### Segment 2 · Rent & payment · medium

> The rent is Rs. 12,000 per month, payable in cash on the first day of every month. No receipts will be issued for cash payments.

Paying without receipts leaves you with no proof that rent was paid if a dispute arises. Key numbers: ₹12,000.

*Ask for:* Pay by bank transfer/UPI or insist on signed receipts.

### Segment 3 · Security deposit · high

> The tenant shall pay a deposit equal to three months' rent. A sum of Rs. 5,000 from the deposit is non-refundable and will be retained towards administrative charges.

Part of what you pay will not come back to you, even if you leave the property in perfect condition. The deposit equals several months of rent, which is a lot of your money held by the landlord. India's Model Tenancy Act, 2021 suggests a cap of two months' rent for homes (only in states that have adopted it). Key numbers: 3 month(s), ₹5,000.

*Ask for:* Ask for the full deposit to be refundable, minus documented damage beyond normal wear and tear.

### Segment 4 · Liability & indemnity · medium

> The tenant must keep the room clean and is responsible for any damage to furniture provided in the room. Common areas will be cleaned by a housekeeper arranged by the owner.

The trained model rates this clause as risky because its wording resembles risky clauses in the training data (driven by “owner”, “for any”, “will be”, “damage to”). Read it carefully and ask what each obligation means for you in practice.

*Ask for:* Ask the landlord to clarify or narrow this clause in writing.

### Segment 5 · Rent & payment · high

> The owner may revise the rent at any time as the owner deems fit, with intimation to the tenant.

This clause lets one side decide on its own, with no objective standard and no way for you to challenge the decision. The rent can be raised without a fixed limit, so you cannot predict what renewal will cost.

*Ask for:* Replace 'sole discretion' with an objective test (e.g. 'reasonable, documented costs') or require mutual agreement.
