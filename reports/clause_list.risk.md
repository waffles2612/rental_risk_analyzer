## Flagged clauses

### Segment 2 · Security deposit · high

> The security deposit of Rs. 1,08,000 shall be refunded after the Tenant vacates, less any deductions the Owner deems necessary.

The security deposit equals roughly 6.0 months of rent. That is a lot of your money held by the landlord, and it is harder to recover in a dispute. For reference, India's Model Tenancy Act, 2021 suggests a cap of two months' rent for residential premises (it applies only in states that have adopted it). The clause says the deposit will be returned but gives no deadline, so the landlord can delay it indefinitely. Key numbers: ₹108,000.

*Ask for:* Negotiate the deposit down to 2-3 months' rent, or ask for interest on the excess.

### Segment 3 · Termination & notice · high

> The Owner may terminate this agreement by giving the Tenant ten days' notice.

The landlord can end the tenancy with under a month's notice, which is very little time to find a new home. You have to give about 60 days' notice to leave, but the landlord only has to give about 10 days. The termination terms are tilted against you. Key numbers: 10 day(s).

*Ask for:* Ask for at least one month's written notice.

### Segment 4 · Termination & notice · high

> The Tenant may terminate this agreement by giving two months' written notice to the Owner.

You have to give about 60 days' notice to leave, but the landlord only has to give about 10 days. The termination terms are tilted against you. Key numbers: 2 month(s).

*Ask for:* Ask for the same notice period for both parties.

### Segment 5 · Renewal & term · medium

> This agreement shall stand automatically renewed for eleven months unless the Tenant gives notice sixty days before expiry.

The agreement renews by itself unless someone gives notice in time. Miss the window and you can be bound for another term, often at a higher rent. Key numbers: 2 month(s), 11 month(s).

*Ask for:* Put the notice deadline in your calendar, or ask for renewal only by mutual written agreement.

### Segment 6 · Renewal & term · high

> On renewal the rent shall be increased by 12% of the last rent paid.

The rent rises by 10% or more, which is above typical annual increases (often around 5-10%). Key numbers: 12%.

*Ask for:* Negotiate a lower cap (e.g. 5%) or a fixed rent for the first renewal.

### Segment 8 · Landlord entry & access · high

> The Owner may visit and inspect the flat at any time without prior notice.

The landlord can come into your home whenever they like, which affects your privacy and security.

*Ask for:* Require at least 24 hours' written notice except in a genuine emergency.

### Segment 9 · Penalties & late fees · high

> Late payment shall attract interest at 2% per month on the amount due.

Interest of 2% or more per month is 24%+ per year, far above normal lending rates. Key numbers: 2%.

*Ask for:* Ask for a modest flat late fee or a low annual interest rate.

### Segment 11 · Use of premises · medium

> Overnight guests are not permitted without the Owner's written approval.

You need permission to have guests, which reaches into your private life in your own home.

*Ask for:* Limit restrictions to long-term additional occupants, not ordinary visitors.
