Rental agreement review · rule-based engine

Find the risky clauses before you sign

Paste an agreement or pick a sample. Every clause is classified, checked against the risk rules, and explained in plain English. The whole agreement is also checked for unequal notice periods, oversized deposits and missing clauses.

Text stays in your browser. For PDF and Word files, and the trained-model engine, use the Python tool in the project.

Automated screening, not legal advice. It catches common one-sided wording; it can miss unusual phrasing and can flag clauses that are fine in context. Have important agreements reviewed by a lawyer before signing.
